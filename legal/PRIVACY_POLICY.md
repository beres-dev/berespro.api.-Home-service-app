This policy is issued under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and
explains how Berespro collects, uses, and protects personal data.

## 1. What we collect
| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, phone, email | Sign-up (Google/phone via Firebase) | Account, contact between parties |
| Approximate location / chosen city | Using the app | Matching jobs by distance |
| Task details, bids, messages, reviews | Using the marketplace | Providing the service |
| KYC: full name, IC last-4 digits, ID document images, selfie | Partner verification | Safety of customers whose homes partners enter; fraud prevention |
| Device push token | If notifications enabled | Job alerts |
| Payment events (amounts, status) | Transactions | Order records. **Card/banking details are collected by our licensed PSP, never by Berespro.** |

We do **not** collect: full IC numbers in our database (last-4 only; document
images are stored separately, see §4), precise background location, or contacts.

## 2. How we use it
Service delivery, matching, safety verification, fraud prevention, dispute
resolution, legal compliance, and service notifications. We do **not** sell
personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with
- **PSP** (payment processing, partner payout KYC) — as required to process payments
- **Firebase/Google** (authentication, push delivery)
- **Cloudflare** (hosting infrastructure)
- The **other party to your job** sees only what is necessary: first name,
  rating, and job-related details. Phone numbers are shared only after a bid is
  accepted.
- Authorities where required by Malaysian law.

## 4. KYC document handling (special category)
ID document images and selfies are stored in a private, access-controlled bucket,
viewable only by authorised reviewers via short-lived signed links, retained for
[6 months] after account closure or as required by law, then deleted. Our database
stores only your name and the last 4 digits of your IC.

## 5. Retention
Account data: while your account is active + [24 months]. Transaction and audit
records: [7 years] (statutory). KYC images: per §4. You may request deletion at
any time (subject to statutory retention).

## 6. Your rights (PDPA)
Access, correction, withdrawal of consent, and limitation of processing. Requests
to [DPO/SUPPORT EMAIL]; we respond within 21 days as required by the PDPA.

## 7. Security
Encryption in transit (TLS), signed session tokens with expiry, rate limiting,
audit logging of sensitive actions, principle-of-least-access for staff, and no
storage of card data on our systems.

## 8. Children
Berespro is not for persons under 18.

## 9. Changes
Material changes will be notified in-app before taking effect.
