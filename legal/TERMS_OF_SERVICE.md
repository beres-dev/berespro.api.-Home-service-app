## 1. What Berespro is (and is not)
Berespro is a **platform that connects** customers who post tasks with independent
partners who bid to perform them. Partners are **independent contractors**, not
employees or agents of Berespro. Berespro does not perform tasks, does not
supervise work, and is not a party to the service contract formed between customer
and partner when a bid is accepted.

## 2. Payments
All payments are processed and **held by a licensed payment service provider**.
Berespro never holds customer funds. On confirmed completion the PSP releases 80%
of the agreed price to the partner; 20% is the platform service fee. The fee is
always displayed to partners before they bid.

## 3. Accounts & eligibility
18+, accurate information, one account per person. Partners must complete identity
verification (KYC) before bidding and payout onboarding with the PSP before their
bids can be accepted.

## 4. Partner obligations
Perform work with reasonable skill and care; hold any licenses the law requires
for the trade (e.g. electrical); arrive as scheduled; communicate through the
platform until a bid is accepted; carry own tools unless agreed otherwise; comply
with the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

## 5. Customer obligations
Describe tasks accurately; provide safe access to the work site; confirm
completion honestly and promptly; pay only through the platform.

## 6. Prohibited
Illegal tasks; tasks requiring licenses the partner does not hold; weapons,
controlled substances; adult services; academic fraud; **off-platform payment to
circumvent fees** (grounds for suspension); harassment; discriminatory refusals;
fake reviews or bid manipulation; more than one account.

## 7. Reviews & content
Reviews must reflect genuine experience. We may remove content that is unlawful,
defamatory, or violates these terms, and provide an in-app mechanism to report it.

## 8. Disputes between users
Handled under the Refund & Cancellation Policy. While a dispute is open the held
payment is frozen. Support's resolution of the held amount is final within the
platform, without limiting either party's rights at law.

## 9. Liability
To the extent permitted by Malaysian law: Berespro's aggregate liability for any
claim is limited to the platform fees earned from the transaction concerned.
Berespro is not liable for the quality of partner work (see §1), though we
facilitate resolution in good faith. Nothing excludes liability that cannot be
excluded by law, including under the Consumer Protection Act 1999 where it applies.
