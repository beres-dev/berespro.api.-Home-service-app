All payments on Berespro are processed and **held by a licensed payment service
provider (PSP)** — Berespro does not hold customer funds. "Refund" below means the
PSP returning the held amount to the customer's original payment method.

## 1. Payment states
| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Held | Customer paid; PSP holds funds; work may begin |
| Released | Customer confirmed completion; PSP paid partner 80%, platform fee 20% |
| Refunded | Held funds returned in full to the customer |

## 2. Cancellation by the customer
- **Before a bid is accepted:** free, no payment exists yet.
- **After acceptance, before work starts:** full refund of the held amount.
  The partner is notified immediately.
- **After work has started:** cancellation triggers a support review. If the partner
  has incurred reasonable, evidenced costs (materials purchased, travel completed),
  support may apportion the held amount. Neither party can force an outcome
  unilaterally once work has started.

## 3. Cancellation by the partner
- Partner may withdraw before starting work: customer receives a full refund and
  the partner's cancellation rate is recorded on their profile.
- Repeated partner cancellations (3 in 30 days) lead to temporary suspension.

## 4. No-shows
- Partner no-show: full refund to the customer; strike recorded against partner.
- Customer no-show (partner arrives, cannot access site): partner may claim a
  call-out portion via dispute, with evidence (timestamped photo/location).

## 5. Disputes
- Either party may open a dispute while a job is in progress. **The held payment is
  frozen** — it cannot be released or refunded until Berespro support resolves it.
- Both parties may submit evidence (photos, chat records) within 3 business days.
- Support resolves within 7 business days: full release, full refund, or a
  documented split. Decisions are recorded in an audit log.

## 6. Completed jobs
Once a customer confirms completion and funds are released, the transaction is
final. Post-completion workmanship issues within 7 days may be raised to support,
who may facilitate a goodwill resolution with the partner; the released payment
itself cannot be reversed by Berespro.

## 7. Chargebacks
Initiating a card chargeback while a Berespro dispute is open may result in
account suspension pending the PSP's chargeback outcome. Genuine unauthorized-
transaction chargebacks are always your right.

## 8. Timeframes
Refunds are initiated by the PSP within 1 business day of the decision; arrival
depends on the payment method (cards 5–14 days, FPX 1–3 days, e-wallet 1–3 days).
